REVIEW DRAFT — 24 targets, 12 fading and 12 rising; MIRI/MRS only; intended Medium program, ETC/APT total pending.

Title:
Does circumnuclear dust remember an AGN's accretion history?

Abstract:
Large luminosity changes in active galactic nuclei provide timed experiments on circumnuclear dust. We propose MIRI/MRS spectroscopy of 24 AGN, divided into 12 fading and 12 rising sources, to test whether delayed illumination of a fixed dust distribution explains their infrared response or whether the emitting distribution must evolve. Every target has SPHEREx spectra and ZTF/WISE light-curve and manifold coverage. The archival optical record is extended with 2026 NGPS spectra. SPHEREx constrains the hot-dust response; MIRI adds the warm continuum, silicate features and spatial information needed to separate nuclear and host emission. Models anchored to the preceding optical and infrared histories predict the warm spectrum at the MIRI epoch. We will compare the measured residuals between rising and fading sources, retaining earlier recoveries and reversals in each history and accounting for light-travel delays, extinction and host light. A reproducible dependence on illumination history would support dust evolution; agreement with fixed dust would constrain structural changes. One MIRI visit per target supplies this test across a balanced sample.
