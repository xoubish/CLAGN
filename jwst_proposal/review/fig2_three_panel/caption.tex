\caption{\textbf{Published examples motivate our experiment across 24 AGN activity histories.}
\textbf{A:} NGC~7469 illustrates hot/warm dust decomposition [15, Fig.~8].
Green/gold shading marks nominal SPHEREx/MRS rest-wavelength access common to
our sample. Black points are data; yellow/red curves are the dust continuum/full
model. Purple ticks mark PAH bands; dotted gold lines mark silicate features.
\textbf{B:} NGC~6552 spectra and 11.6~$\mu$m maps demonstrate separation of
central and surrounding emission [28, Fig.~4], constraining resolved host light.
\textbf{C:} NGC~4151 illustrates a delayed, broadened warm-dust response
[23, Fig.~15]: blue is optical emission, red points are 10.5~$\mu$m measurements,
and light-blue curves are fitted responses. This motivates our history-dependent
spectral test; our single visit does not measure a lag. Wavelengths are rest-frame
in A and observed-frame in B. Longer-wavelength data and ancillary fit labels
are omitted from C.}
