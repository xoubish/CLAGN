\caption{\textbf{Published examples demonstrate the diagnostics behind our experiment.}
\textbf{A:} NGC~7469 illustrates hot/warm dust decomposition [15, Fig.~8].
Green and gold shading marks nominal SPHEREx and MRS rest-wavelength access
common to our sample. Black points are data; yellow/red curves are the dust
continuum/full model. Purple ticks locate PAH bands; dotted gold lines mark
silicate features.
\textbf{B:} NGC~6552 spectra and 11.6~$\mu$m maps demonstrate separation of
central and surrounding emission [28, Fig.~4], enabling host constraints where
extended emission is resolved.
\textbf{C:} NGC~4151 demonstrates a delayed, broadened warm-dust response
[23, Fig.~15]: blue is optical emission, red points are 10.5~$\mu$m measurements,
and light-blue curves are fitted responses. This motivates testing whether our
warm spectra depend on prior activity at comparable current power.
The illustrated timescales belong to NGC~4151; our single visit does not measure
a lag. Wavelengths are rest-frame in A and observed-frame in B.
Longer-wavelength photometry and ancillary fit labels are omitted from C.}
