We request 67.0 hours of MIRI Medium Resolution Spectroscopy (MRS) for 24 nearby (z < 0.3) changing-look and strongly variable AGN with recorded rises, declines, flares and recoveries. Each nucleus will be observed once in all three MRS grating settings, providing approximately 4.9-27.9 micron coverage of the warm continuum, both silicate features, PAH bands and diagnostic emission lines. Science observations use nuclear target acquisition, four-point dithers and FULL/FASTR1 readout. The requested continuum sensitivity is S/N = 50 per R = 100 bin near rest-frame 5 and 12 microns and S/N = 30 near 18 microns. The faintest W3 target, J083826.50+371906.7, receives additional exposure in setting B.

Each science visit has an equal-depth observation of a nearby blank-sky field selected using WISE and 2MASS data. Separate sky observations preserve extended host emission for spatial and spectral decomposition. Sky visits use four-point extended-source dithers without target acquisition. Each science/sky pair is linked as a consecutive, noninterruptible sequence to provide a contemporaneous background. The programme comprises 24 science and 24 sky visits, with 17.00 hours of science-plus-sky integration and 67.0 hours total charged time. No fixed-date constraints, coordinated parallels or joint-facility time are requested.
