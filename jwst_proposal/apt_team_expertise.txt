Team Expertise and Background:

Our team combines expertise in AGN variability, infrared dust diagnostics, JWST spectroscopy, and survey calibration. PI Shoubaneh Hemmati has developed machine-learning analyses of multiband AGN light curves and will coordinate sample characterization, reconstruction of activity histories, and the joint tests of dust memory and evolution. Irene Shivaei brings experience from the JWST/MIRI development and commissioning teams and direct MIRI/MRS analysis of PAH emission through PAHSPECS; she will guide MRS reduction, spectral decomposition, and interpretation of resolved host dust. George Helou brings extensive experience in infrared emission, dust heating, and separating star-formation and AGN contributions, supporting the physical interpretation of the warm continuum and aromatic features.

Jessica Krick brings Spitzer/IRAC calibration expertise, including infrared photometry of JWST calibration stars, and will support photometric consistency checks and assessment of background and calibration uncertainties. Andreas Faisst combines SPHEREx pipeline and dust-modeling experience with leadership of JWST galaxy surveys; he will support SPHEREx spectral validation and joint modeling of nuclear and host emission. David Shupe brings survey-pipeline experience in ZTF astrometric calibration, source matching, and relative photometry, supporting light-curve quality control and reproducible analysis. Yu-Heng Lin brings experience in quasar spectroscopy and emission-line diagnostics and will contribute to optical spectral-state measurements and comparison with the infrared diagnostics. These complementary contributions cover the path from calibrated observations and activity histories to tests of delayed heating and dust replenishment.
