The allocated nights

All times are Palomar local time (PDT), including October. The default view shows the list prepared for each night; other usable nights remain in each target’s details. The Moon is above the horizon throughout these windows.

How to use this review

First compare W1 behavior with the dates and broad-line states of earlier spectra. Then ask what a new spectrum could establish: a first transition, persistence, recurrence, or a test of an uncertain prediction. No on/off forecast or discovery probability has been assigned.

Brightness is historical. Moon separation and airmass are geometric cuts, not a guarantee of line S/N. Exposures need current brightness and line-specific sensitivity estimates.

The manifold uses normalized W1 light-curve shapes; its axes are not a clock. The two regions have different selection and validation histories.
