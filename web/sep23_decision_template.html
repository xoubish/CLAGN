Summary · one row per candidate

S/N is the predicted continuum S/N per Å near observed Hβ for 2 × 300 s with the 1.5″ slit and 2×3 binning, from the official ETC with seeing scaled to airmass and a moonlit sky at each candidate start. "Best" gives the best slot and its time; "ok" counts slots at or above the floor of 5. CL fraction is the manifold changing-look neighbour fraction; review score is the existing selection utility, not a probability.

Predicted S/N uses archival continuum brightness, a moonlit-sky model and seeing scaled to the target airmass. It is not a guarantee of broad-Hβ significance. Spectral overlays are not aperture-matched or host-subtracted; no on/off classification follows from a raw flux offset. The manifold is a selection prior, not a clock.
