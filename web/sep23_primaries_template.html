Start means beginning acquisition, not opening the shutter. Standard-star exposures are initial settings requiring saturation checks. Substitute a backup into the sequence; the backup CSV is not an additional automatic observing run.

Exposure estimates use archival continuum, a moonlit-sky model and seeing scaled to the target airmass. They are not a guarantee of broad-Hβ significance. Spectral overlays are not aperture-matched or host-subtracted; no on/off classification follows from a raw flux offset. The manifold is a selection prior, not a clock.
