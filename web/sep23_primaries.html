Planned visits include 20 minutes of integration and 10 minutes for acquisition, readout and margin. Standards bookend the sequence; a 10-minute contingency interval precedes P08. Check current brightness, sky, slit orientation and quicklook spectra before extending an exposure.

Intended order · visit start to finish

Start means beginning acquisition, not opening the shutter. Standard-star exposures are initial settings requiring saturation checks. Substitute a backup into the sequence; the backup CSV is not an additional automatic observing run.

Exposure estimates use archival continuum and an assumed bright sky. They are not a guarantee of broad-Hβ significance. Spectral overlays are not aperture-matched or host-subtracted; no on/off classification follows from a raw flux offset. The manifold is a selection prior, not a clock.

Centered on the target coordinates. North up; east left. Inspect the source and intended slit position angle.
